# Onboarding feedback

## 2026-10-28, first outside tester (phone)

- "Good remote?" with Yes / No meant nothing to them. Fixed the same day: "Design your own TV remote." with Make it mine / It's perfect.
- They read the page as a proposal for a **phone app**: "I don't want to use a tiny remote on my phone." Nothing on screen says the remote is a physical object.
- They tried Size and dragged things, but could not tell whether this was a full-screen app, a web page, or something with a server.
- They prefer physical buttons, so they felt they were not the audience. They are, in fact, the audience; the page did not tell them.
- With blank caps they could not judge a layout: "is it numbers or app buttons or up and down?" Key legends are in the works (owner).
- Ergonomics: on a real remote the common keys belong where the thumb rests; they wondered how that maps to a phone. Again the phone-app reading.
- Owner's read: the progressive disclosure is too slow. The tester never reached Build or Buy, which is where the page shows it is real hardware.
